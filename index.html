Google Sheets backend

Workbook template and Apps Script starter are included in the project. The app currently saves locally on your device. Live Google Sheets synchronisation is not switched on in this build.

For private financial information, the sync service must require your Google sign-in and enforce access checks. Do not publish a sheet or API endpoint to “Anyone”. Never put a secret key in the public GitHub client code.
